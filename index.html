How to keep Umster happy

Umster has a whole little life of naps, moods and whims. Here's what you'll be doing together.

🤏

Squish from any side

One finger or five, from the top, the sides or at an angle. A light touch is gentle; press in to go deeper.

🎭

Whims and feelings

Every few hours Umster feels a little different: cuddly, bouncy, dramatic, shy or ticklish. Squish too hard and it's "Eep!"

🔔

Answer the pings

A few times a day Umster wants attention, and you've got 60 seconds. Miss it, and there will be sulking.

💋

Snacks, flowers and kisses

Well-timed snacks, a yellow flower for sudden sleepy spells, and forehead kisses that vacuum away worries when thunder rolls in.

🌙

Lives on your clock

Bedtime is around 10:30 pm, with naps in between. Very rarely, there are midnight zoomies until 2:30 am.

😤

Moods with reasons

Sometimes grumpy out of nowhere. A snack might get Umster talking, and making up takes a little luck.

Questions

How much does Umster cost?

Umster is free to download. Optional in-app purchases add sunflower seeds or extra hamster slots, and both can also be earned by playing.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in: your hamsters and progress are saved on your phone.

What happens if I miss a ping?

Umster sulks for a couple of hours. Turn on notifications so you don't miss the next one.

Who is Umster for?

Umster is made for teens and adults, ages 13 and up.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

When can I play?

Umster is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Check back here, or write to support@umstergames.com.
