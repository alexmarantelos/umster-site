Umster Support

Umster is a squishy little hamster with big feelings who lives on your phone's clock. Squish her (gently), feed her snacks, answer when she calls and keep her happy. Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, email support@umstergames.com.

Playing

Why is she asleep?

Umster follows your phone's local time. She heads to bed around 10:30 pm, sleeps 9 to 10 hours, and takes one to three naps during the day.

Squishing her while she sleeps wakes her up grumpy, though once in a while she loves a sleepy squish. A forehead kiss won't wake her; she just smiles in her sleep. On very rare nights she gets the midnight zoomies and stays up until about 2:30 am.

What's an attention ping?

Three to five times a day, Umster asks for attention. You have one minute to answer: squish her, tap Give attention! or tap the notification. If you miss it, she feels ignored and sulks for two hours.

A brand-new hamster gets an hour to settle in before the first ping.

Why is she grumpy, huffy, bloated or hiding?
  • Grumpy: you missed a ping, you woke her up, or she's just in a mood. She won't say why at first. Offer a snack, a paw massage or a forehead kiss and she'll tell you what's wrong. Then tap Make up. It doesn't always work: it gets easier once she's explained, as time passes and as your bond grows, and she needs 15 minutes of space between tries.
  • Huffy: you squished harder than she likes right now. She squeaks "Eep!" and needs about 8 minutes.
  • Bloated: three snacks within an hour is too many. She's bloated for an hour; a tummy rub from the shop helps.
  • Hiding: she's gone about 8 waking hours without a snack. Offer one to coax her out of bed.
  • Tired: some days she suddenly gets tired. Give her a yellow flower 🌼. She may still need a short nap.
  • Worried: there's thunder outside her window. A forehead kiss clears the sky.
How do squishes and squish levels work?

Squish her with one finger or several, from any side. Every few hours her whim changes (cuddly, bouncy, dramatic, shy or ticklish), and that decides how deep a squish she enjoys. A cuddly Umster loves a long, deep squish; a ticklish one prefers the lightest boop.

Good care (answered pings, well-timed snacks, happy squishes, treats and kisses) raises her squish level from 1 to 5. Each level lets you squish a little deeper.

What are seeds, goals and streaks?

Sunflower seeds 🌻 are Umster's currency. You earn them from daily goals, stickers, level-ups and good care, and spend them in the shop on food, fun, wellbeing treats and accessories.

Every day brings three goals. Finish all three to grow your streak and earn a bonus.

How do I get more hamsters?

Open the hamster house (Hamsters). There are five breeds: Golden, Snowy, Cocoa, Panda and Peach. You can have up to five hamsters.

To earn room for another hamster, get the one you're playing with to Squish Lv 5 while you hold a 14-day streak. You can also buy an extra slot. Hamsters you're not playing with nap in the house, and time stands still for them until you come back.

Notifications

What notifications does Umster send?

If you allow them, Umster lets you know when she wants attention, gets tired, gets hungry or hides in bed, gets suddenly grumpy, hears thunder, or has the midnight zoomies. Your phone schedules them itself; nothing is sent from a server.

Because of the zoomies, a rare notification can arrive as late as about 2:30 am. Focus or Do Not Disturb will keep things quiet if you'd rather sleep.

How do I turn notifications on or off?
  • iPhone: Settings › Notifications › Umster.
  • Android: Settings › Apps › Umster › Notifications (names vary a little by phone). On Android 12 and later, Umster may also ask you to allow "Alarms & reminders" so pings arrive on the minute.

You can play without notifications. You might just miss a ping.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for Umster and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them. On Android, also check that "Alarms & reminders" is allowed and that battery saving isn't restricting Umster.

Umster schedules notifications when you leave the app, so open it once after changing a setting.

Purchases

What can I buy?

Umster is free to play. Optional purchases are seed packs (100 seeds for $0.99, 600 for $4.99, 1,500 for $9.99) and extra hamster slots ($1.99 each, up to four). Prices are in US dollars, and the store shows the final price before you pay.

Everything in the shop costs seeds, and you can earn all the seeds you need by playing. Extra hamster slots can be earned too.

How do I restore my purchases?

Extra hamster slots are yours to keep. On a new phone, or after reinstalling Umster, sign in to the store with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Umster's Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom.

Seed packs can't be restored. Seeds go into your balance on your phone, so they're lost if you delete the app.

Something went wrong with a purchase, or I'd like a refund.

Apple and Google handle payments, so refunds go through them: on iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, use your Google Play order history.

If you paid but didn't get your seeds or hamster slot, email us with the date, the item and the order number from your receipt, and we'll help you sort it out.

Your hamster and your data

I deleted the app or got a new phone. Where's my hamster?

Your hamsters, seeds and progress are saved only on your phone. We don't keep a copy, so we can't bring them back.

If you restore your whole phone from a backup (iCloud or your computer on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your game may come back with it. Extra hamster slots you bought can be restored (see above), but the hamsters in them start fresh.

Can I play on two phones?

Each phone has its own Umster. There's no account, so progress doesn't sync between devices.

How do I send feedback?

Tap 💬 at the top of the main screen, pick Idea, Bug, Love or Other, write up to 1,000 characters and tap Send. Every note gets read.

Notes don't include your name or contact details, so we can't reply to them. If you'd like an answer, email us instead. Please leave personal details out of notes. You can see exactly what's sent with each note in the privacy policy.

Does Umster have ads or collect my data?

No ads, no account and no tracking. Your game stays on your phone, and the only information Umster sends us is feedback you choose to send. The privacy policy has the details.

Who is Umster for?

Umster is made for teens and adults, ages 13 and up.

Still stuck?

Email support@umstergames.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened, and we'll get back to you.
