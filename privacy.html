Umster Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Umster is a hamster game for iPhone and Android. It's made by an independent developer, Ioannis Alexandros Marantelos, who publishes it on the App Store under that name and on Google Play as Umster Game Studios. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that developer.

The short version

  • There's no account and no sign-in. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your hamsters, seeds and progress are saved only on your phone.
  • If you choose to send feedback, we get your note and a few game and device details, but never your name or an ID. We use it only to make Umster better.
  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

What stays on your phone

Everything you do in the game is saved only on your phone: your hamsters and the names you give them, your sunflower seeds, goals, streak, stickers, shop items and any hamster slots you've unlocked. None of it is sent to us, and we can't see it. Deleting Umster deletes it.

If your phone backs up its apps (for example with iCloud or Google backup), that backup may include Umster's game data. Apple or Google keeps those backups under your account, and we can't access them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Umster can tell you when your hamster wants attention, gets tired, gets hungry, gets grumpy, hears thunder or has the midnight zoomies. These are local notifications: your phone schedules and shows them itself. There's no push server, and nothing about your notifications is sent to us. On Android, Umster may also ask to use "Alarms & reminders" so they arrive on time. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

What we don't collect

Umster doesn't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. It doesn't use your phone's advertising ID or any other device identifier. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Feedback you choose to send

The 💬 button in the game opens a feedback form. Sending a note is entirely up to you. When you tap Send, we receive:

  • the kind of note you picked (idea, bug, love or other);
  • what you wrote, up to 1,000 characters;
  • the app version, whether your phone runs iOS or Android, and its operating system version;
  • your hamster's squish level, your streak and how many hamsters you have;
  • the time the note arrived, and a random code made for that note alone so it isn't saved twice.

A note doesn't include your name, email address, account or any identifier that could link it to you, to your phone or to your other notes. Please don't write personal details, such as your name, contact details or social media handles, in a note. If you're offline when you send one, it waits on your phone and goes out the next time you open Umster.

Like anything sent over the internet, a note reaches our database provider from your phone's IP address. The provider may keep IP addresses in its server logs for a short time, for security and troubleshooting. We don't store your IP address with your note, and we don't use it to identify you.

How we use feedback

We use notes only to fix bugs and decide how to make Umster better. We don't use them for advertising or to build profiles, we don't try to link them to who you are, and we never sell them.

Each night an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) reads the new notes and writes a summary for the developer: what players are asking for, which bugs to look at and what people love. The summary is saved, together with the notes themselves, as an issue in our private code repository on GitHub. Anything in a note that looks like an email address, phone number or social media handle is removed first. Claude only summarizes notes. It doesn't reply to you or make decisions about you.

Purchases

Seed packs and extra hamster slots are sold through the App Store and Google Play, using Apple's and Google's own checkout. Apple or Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy, and we never see your payment details, such as your card number. The store sends Umster a confirmation on your phone so the game can add your seeds or hamster slot. Umster doesn't send any purchase information to our servers.

Apple and Google give us sales reports for bookkeeping and taxes (for example, what was bought, when, for how much and in which region). We use them only for that, and to help if you contact us about a purchase.

Who handles information for us

  • Supabase, Inc. stores feedback notes in a database in the United States.
  • Anthropic, PBC provides the AI assistant (Claude) that summarizes new notes.
  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the private repository where the summaries are kept.

Each of them handles notes only to provide its service to us, under terms that require it to keep the information secure and to protect it at least as well as this policy describes. Apple and Google distribute Umster and handle purchases under their own privacy policies.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We'll only disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Game data stays on your phone until you delete Umster.
  • Feedback notes and their summaries are kept until they're no longer needed to improve Umster, and then deleted. We'll delete a note sooner if you ask.
  • Our providers keep their server logs for a short time, under their own schedules.

Your choices

  • Sending feedback is up to you. The game works the same either way.
  • You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings. The game still works; you might just miss a ping.
  • To erase your game data, delete Umster from your phone.
  • To delete a note you sent, email support@umstergames.com and tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said. Notes aren't linked to you, so those details are the only way we can find yours. We'll delete it from our database and from the summaries.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. Email us and we'll help. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Children

Umster is made for teens and adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has sent us a note with personal information in it, email support@umstergames.com and we'll delete it.

Keeping information safe

Notes travel to our database over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The app can only add notes; it can't read them back. Reading notes takes a secret key that only the developer's own tools have. No system is perfectly secure, so we keep what we collect small to begin with.

Do Not Track

Umster and this website don't track you across other apps or websites, so there's nothing for a browser's Do Not Track signal to switch off.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. Like most websites, its host may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Where information is stored

Umster is available in the United States, and feedback notes are stored in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what Umster collects (for example, if we ever add ads), we'll update this policy before that change reaches the app and mention it in the app's update notes.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: support@umstergames.com
